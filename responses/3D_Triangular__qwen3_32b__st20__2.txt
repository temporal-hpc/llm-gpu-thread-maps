To determine the coordinates $(x, y)$ corresponding to a given number $ n $, we follow a structured approach involving the concept of **layers** and a **position-based mapping** within those layers.

---

### Step 1: Determine the Layer $ z $

Each number $ n $ belongs to a **layer** defined by the formula:

$$
S(z) = \frac{z(z+1)(z+2)}{6}
$$

This represents the **starting index** of layer $ z $. We find the **largest integer $ z $** such that:

$$
S(z) \leq n
$$

This can be done using binary search or by solving the cubic inequality.

---

### Step 2: Compute the Position within the Layer

Once the layer $ z $ is determined, compute the **position within the layer**:

$$
\text{pos} = n - S(z)
$$

This position $ \text{pos} $ tells us how far into the layer $ z $ the number $ n $ is.

---

### Step 3: Determine the $ y $-Coordinate

Within the layer, the elements are arranged in a sequence based on the values of $ y $, starting from 0 up to $ z $. The number of elements up to and including a certain $ y $ is given by:

$$
T(y) = \frac{y(y+1)}{2}
$$

We find the **maximum integer $ y $** such that:

$$
T(y) \leq \text{pos}
$$

This can be computed as:

$$
y = \left\lfloor \frac{-1 + \sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot \text{pos}}}{2} \right\rfloor
$$

---

### Step 4: Determine the $ x $-Coordinate

Finally, the $ x $-coordinate is computed as the **remainder** after accounting for the elements up to $ y $:

$$
x = \text{pos} - T(y)
$$

---

### Final Result

The coordinates corresponding to the number $ n $ are:

$$
\boxed{(x, y)}
$$

Where:

- $ z $ is the layer number such that $ \frac{z(z+1)(z+2)}{6} \leq n $,
- $ \text{pos} = n - \frac{z(z+1)(z+2)}{6} $,
- $ y = \left\lfloor \frac{-1 + \sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot \text{pos}}}{2} \right\rfloor $,
- $ x = \text{pos} - \frac{y(y+1)}{2} $

---

### Example

Let’s compute the coordinates for $ n = 7 $:

1. **Find $ z $:**  
   $ S(2) = \frac{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4}{6} = 4 $, $ S(3) = 10 $.  
   So $ z = 2 $.

2. **Compute pos:**  
   $ \text{pos} = 7 - 4 = 3 $

3. **Find $ y $:**  
   $ y = \left\lfloor \frac{-1 + \sqrt{1 + 8 \cdot 3}}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{-1 + \sqrt{25}}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{4}{2} \right\rfloor = 2 $

4. **Find $ x $:**  
   $ x = 3 - \frac{2 \cdot 3}{2} = 3 - 3 = 0 $

**Result:**  
$$
\boxed{(0, 2)}
$$
