To map each non-negative integer $ n $ to a coordinate in a 2D grid, we can use a triangular number-based approach. This method ensures a unique and systematic mapping from $ n \in \mathbb{N}_0 $ to a coordinate $ (x, y) \in \mathbb{N}_0^2 $.

---

### **Step-by-Step Explanation**

1. **Triangular Number Insight**:
   - The triangular number $ T_y = \frac{y(y+1)}{2} $ represents the total number of integers that can be mapped to coordinates with $ y $-coordinate less than or equal to $ y $.
   - For a given $ n $, we want to find the largest $ y $ such that $ T_y \leq n $. This $ y $ determines the row in the grid.

2. **Compute $ y $**:
   - Solve the inequality $ \frac{y(y+1)}{2} \leq n $.
   - This leads to the formula:
     $$
     y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
     $$
   - This gives the largest integer $ y $ such that the triangular number $ T_y $ is less than or equal to $ n $.

3. **Compute $ x $**:
   - Once $ y $ is determined, the remaining value $ x $ is:
     $$
     x = n - \frac{y(y+1)}{2}
     $$
   - This gives the position within the row corresponding to $ y $.

---

### **Final Mapping Formula**

Given a non-negative integer $ n $, the corresponding coordinate $ (x, y) $ is:

$$
\boxed{
\left( n - \frac{y(y+1)}{2},\ y \right)
}
$$

where

$$
y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
$$

---

### **Example**

Let’s compute the coordinate for $ n = 5 $:

- $ \sqrt{8 \cdot 5 + 1} = \sqrt{41} \approx 6.4 $
- $ y = \left\lfloor \frac{6.4 - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor 2.7 \right\rfloor = 2 $
- $ x = 5 - \frac{2 \cdot 3}{2} = 5 - 3 = 2 $
- So, the coordinate is $ (2, 2) $

---

This method ensures a one-to-one mapping from non-negative integers to 2D coordinates in a triangular grid.
