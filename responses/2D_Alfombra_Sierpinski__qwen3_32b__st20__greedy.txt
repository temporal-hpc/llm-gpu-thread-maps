To determine the coordinates of a number $ n $ in a spiral grid, we can use a structured approach based on the concept of layers in the spiral. Here's a step-by-step breakdown of the process:

---

### **Step 1: Determine the Layer $ l $**
Each layer $ l $ in the spiral corresponds to a square ring around the center. The number of elements in each layer increases as the square of the odd numbers. The formula to find the layer $ l $ for a given $ n $ is:

$$
l = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{n} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor + 1
$$

This gives the smallest integer $ l $ such that $ (2l - 1)^2 < n \leq (2l + 1)^2 $.

---

### **Step 2: Compute the Previous Corner**
The previous corner is the largest square number less than $ n $, which is:

$$
\text{previous\_corner} = (2l - 1)^2
$$

---

### **Step 3: Compute the Offset $ m $**
The offset $ m $ is the number of steps from the previous corner to $ n $:

$$
m = n - \text{previous\_corner}
$$

---

### **Step 4: Determine the Side and Position**
Each layer has four sides, each with $ 2l $ steps. The side is determined by:

$$
\text{side} = \left\lfloor \frac{m}{2l} \right\rfloor
$$

The remainder $ r $ gives the position along the side:

$$
r = m \mod 2l
$$

---

### **Step 5: Compute the Coordinates**
Based on the side and remainder, the coordinates are computed as follows:

- **Side 0 (Right):**  
  $$
  x = l, \quad y = -l + r
  $$

- **Side 1 (Top):**  
  $$
  x = l - r, \quad y = l
  $$

- **Side 2 (Left):**  
  $$
  x = -l, \quad y = l - r
  $$

- **Side 3 (Bottom):**  
  $$
  x = -l + r, \quad y = -l
  $$

---

### **Example: $ n = 2 $**

1. **Layer $ l $:**
   $$
   l = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{2} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = \left\lfloor \frac{1.414 - 1}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = \left\lfloor 0.207 \right\rfloor + 1 = 0 + 1 = 1
   $$

2. **Previous Corner:**
   $$
   \text{previous\_corner} = (2l - 1)^2 = (2 \cdot 1 - 1)^2 = 1^2 = 1
   $$

3. **Offset $ m $:**
   $$
   m = n - \text{previous\_corner} = 2 - 1 = 1
   $$

4. **Side and Remainder:**
   $$
   \text{side} = \left\lfloor \frac{1}{2} \right\rfloor = 0, \quad r = 1 \mod 2 = 1
   $$

5. **Coordinates (Side 0):**
   $$
   x = l = 1, \quad y = -l + r = -1 + 1 = 0
   $$

Wait! This gives $ (1, 0) $, but we know $ n = 2 $ should be at $ (1, 1) $. This discrepancy arises because the formula assumes the spiral starts at $ (0, 0) $, but in reality, the spiral starts at $ (0, 0) $, and the first number $ n = 1 $ is at $ (0, 0) $, $ n = 2 $ is at $ (1, 0) $, and $ n = 3 $ is at $ (1, 1) $.

So, the correct coordinates for $ n = 2 $ are:

$$
\boxed{(1, 0)}
$$

---

### **Final Answer:**
$$
\boxed{(1, 0)}
