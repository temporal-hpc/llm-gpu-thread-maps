To determine how many points in the given structure have a **y-coordinate equal to 2013**, we need to analyze the pattern of how points are distributed in the coordinate plane.

---

### Pattern Analysis

From the problem and earlier observations, we deduce that for any given **y**, the number of points that have that **y-coordinate** is:

$$
\text{count}(y) = 2^k
$$

where $ k $ is the **largest integer** such that:

$$
2^k \leq y + 1
$$

This can be rewritten as:

$$
k = \left\lfloor \log_2(y + 1) \right\rfloor
$$

So, the number of points with a given y-coordinate is:

$$
\text{count}(y) = 2^{\left\lfloor \log_2(y + 1) \right\rfloor}
$$

---

### Applying the Formula to $ y = 2013 $

We compute:

$$
y + 1 = 2013 + 1 = 2014
$$

Now, we find the largest integer $ k $ such that $ 2^k \leq 2014 $.

We know:

- $ 2^{10} = 1024 $
- $ 2^{11} = 2048 $

Since $ 2048 > 2014 $, the largest $ k $ is:

$$
k = 10
$$

Thus, the number of points with y-coordinate 2013 is:

$$
2^{10} = 1024
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{1024}
