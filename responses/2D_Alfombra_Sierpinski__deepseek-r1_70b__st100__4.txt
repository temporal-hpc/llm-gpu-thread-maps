**Step-by-Step Explanation:**

1. **Identify Movement Pattern:**
   - For \( n = 0 \) to \( 2 \), \( y = 0 \) and \( x \) increments from 0 to 2.

   - For \( y = 2 \), \( x \) increments fully from 0 to 2 over three steps (\( n = 5, 6, 7 \)).

2. **Determine the Formula:**
   - The pattern suggests that for each even \( y \), \( x \) increases through all values, while for odd \( y \), \( x \) takes every second value.
   - This can be translated into a formula where \( y \) increments after every 3 steps for even levels and every 2 steps for odd levels.
