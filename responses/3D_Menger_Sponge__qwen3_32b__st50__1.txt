To determine the value of $ z $ based on the value of $ n $, we are given the condition that $ z = 1 $ when $ n \mod 20 \in [8, 19] $, and $ z = 0 $ otherwise.

---

### Step 1: Understanding the Condition

Let $ m = n \mod 20 $, which gives the remainder when $ n $ is divided by 20. We are interested in the cases where $ m \in [8, 19] $. This corresponds to the second and third octants of the 20-unit range.

We want to construct a formula using the **floor function** to express this condition.

---

### Step 2: Constructing the Formula

We can use the floor function to detect when $ m $ is in the desired range:

- $ \left\lfloor \frac{m}{8} \right\rfloor $ gives:
  - 0 for $ m = 0 $ to $ 7 $,
  - 1 for $ m = 8 $ to $ 15 $,
  - 2 for $ m = 16 $ to $ 19 $.

- $ \left\lfloor \frac{m}{16} \right\rfloor $ gives:
  - 0 for $ m = 0 $ to $ 15 $,
  - 1 for $ m = 16 $ to $ 19 $.

Thus, the difference:

$$
\left\lfloor \frac{m}{8} \right\rfloor - \left\lfloor \frac{m}{16} \right\rfloor
$$

will be:

- 0 for $ m = 0 $ to $ 7 $,
- 1 for $ m = 8 $ to $ 15 $,
- 1 for $ m = 16 $ to $ 19 $.

This matches the desired condition where $ z = 1 $ for $ m \in [8, 19] $, and $ z = 0 $ otherwise.

---

### Step 3: Final Formula

Since $ m = n \mod 20 $, the final expression for $ z $ is:

$$
z = \left\lfloor \frac{n \bmod 20}{8} \right\rfloor - \left\lfloor \frac{n \bmod 20}{16} \right\rfloor
$$

This is a compact and accurate representation using only floor functions and modular arithmetic.

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\left\lfloor \frac{n \bmod 20}{8} \right\rfloor - \left\lfloor \frac{n \bmod 20}{16} \right\rfloor}
