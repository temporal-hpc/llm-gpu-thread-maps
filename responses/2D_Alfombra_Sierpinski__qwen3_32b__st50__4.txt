To calculate the coordinates of a number `n` in a **square spiral** (like the Ulam spiral), follow this structured approach:

---

### **Step 1: Determine the Layer**
The layer `m` of the spiral is calculated using:
$$
m = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{n} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
$$
This gives the ring number where `n` resides.

---

### **Step 2: Compute Side Length and Starting Value**
- **Side length**:  
  $$
  s = 2m + 1
  $$
- **Starting value of the layer**:  
  $$
  \text{start} = (s - 2)^2 + 1
  $$

---

### **Step 3: Find the Side and Offset**
- **Offset within the layer**:  
  $$
  \text{offset} = n - \text{start}
  $$
- The layer has four sides of length `2m`. Determine which side `offset` falls on:
  - **Right side**: `0 ≤ offset < 2m`
  - **Top side**: `2m ≤ offset < 4m`
  - **Left side**: `4m ≤ offset < 6m`
  - **Bottom side**: `6m ≤ offset < 8m`

---

### **Step 4: Compute Coordinates Based on the Side**
Let `corner = m` (the distance from the origin to the corner of the layer). The coordinates are derived as follows:

- **Right side** (`0 ≤ offset < 2m`):  
  $$
  x = m, \quad y = -m + \text{offset}
  $$
- **Top side** (`2m ≤ offset < 4m`):  
  $$
  x = m - (\text{offset} - 2m), \quad y = m
  $$
- **Left side** (`4m ≤ offset < 6m`):  
  $$
  x = -m, \quad y = m - (\text{offset} - 4m)
  $$
- **Bottom side** (`6m ≤ offset < 8m`):  
  $$
  x = -m + (\text{offset} - 6m), \quad y = -m
  $$

---

### **Example: n = 8**
1. **Layer**:  
   $$
   m = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{2.828 - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor 0.914 \right\rfloor = 0
   $$
   Wait! This is incorrect. The correct calculation for `m` should use:
   $$
   m = \left\lceil \frac{\sqrt{n} - 1}{2} \right\rceil
   $$
   For `n = 8`:
   $$
   m = \left\lceil \frac{\sqrt{8} - 1}{2} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{2.828 - 1}{2} \right\rceil = \left\lceil 0.914 \right\rceil = 1
   $$

2. **Side length**:  
   $$
   s = 2m + 1 = 3
   $$

3. **Start of layer**:  
   $$
   \text{start} = (s - 2)^2 + 1 = 1^2 + 1 = 2
   $$

4. **Offset**:  
   $$
   \text{offset} = 8 - 2 = 6
   $$

5. **Side**:  
   Since `offset = 6` and `2m = 2`, it falls on the **bottom side** (`6m = 6`).

6. **Coordinates**:  
   $$
   x = -m + (\text{offset} - 6m) = -1 + (6 - 6) = -1, \quad y = -m = -1
   $$
   So, the coordinates are **(-1, -1)**.

---

### **Final Answer**
For `n = 8`, the coordinates in a square spiral are **(-1, -1)**.
