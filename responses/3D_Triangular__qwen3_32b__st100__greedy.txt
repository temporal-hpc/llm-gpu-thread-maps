To map an index `m` (starting from 0) to a coordinate `(x, y)` in a triangular grid where the elements are ordered as follows:

```
(0,0)
(0,1), (1,1)
(0,2), (1,2), (2,2)
(0,3), (1,3), (2,3), (3,3)
...
```

You can use the following method:

---

### **Step-by-Step Explanation**

1. **Determine the Row `y`:**

   The row `y` corresponds to the largest integer such that the sum of the first `y+1` natural numbers is less than or equal to `m`. This is equivalent to solving the inequality:

   $$
   \frac{y(y+1)}{2} \leq m
   $$

   Solving this quadratic inequality gives:

   $$
   y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8m + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
   $$

2. **Determine the Column `x`:**

   Once `y` is known, the column `x` is calculated as the offset within the row:

   $$
   x = m - \frac{y(y+1)}{2}
   $$

---

### **Final Formula**

Given an index `m` (starting from 0), the coordinates `(x, y)` are:

$$
y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8m + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
$$
$$
x = m - \frac{y(y+1)}{2}
$$

---

### **Example**

For `m = 5`:

- $ y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8 \cdot 5 + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{41} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{6.4 - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = 2 $
- $ x = 5 - \frac{2 \cdot 3}{2} = 5 - 3 = 2 $
- Result: `(x, y) = (2, 2)`

---

### **Edge Cases**

- **`m = 0`:**  
  $ y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = 0 $,  
  $ x = 0 - 0 = 0 $,  
  Result: `(0, 0)`

- **`m = 1`:**  
  $ y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{9} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = 1 $,  
  $ x = 1 - \frac{1 \cdot 2}{2} = 0 $,  
  Result: `(0, 1)`

---

### **Conclusion**

This method efficiently maps an index `m` to a coordinate `(x, y)` in a triangular grid, where each row `y` contains `y + 1` elements. The formula is derived from solving a quadratic equation and works for all non-negative integers `m`.
