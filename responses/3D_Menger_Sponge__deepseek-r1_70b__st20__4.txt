   - Calculate the cumulative number of points up to each level until you find the level `z` such that the total points just exceed `n`.

2. **Calculate the Position within the Level (`m`):**
   - Once the correct level is identified, compute `m = n - T(z)`, where `T(z)` is the cumulative number of points up to level `z`.

3. **Determine Coordinates (x, y) Based on `z` and `m`:**

     - For `m` values 0-7, map `m` to `(x, y)` using specific rules for each range of `m`.
   - If `z` is odd:
     - For `m` values 0-3, determine `(x, y)` by checking if `m` falls within the first or second half and whether it's even or odd.
