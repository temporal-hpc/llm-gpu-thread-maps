To determine the 3D coordinates $(x, y, z)$ for a given integer $n$, we observe a specific pattern in how the coordinates are generated. The sequence is structured such that for each fixed $z$, the coordinates $(x, y)$ follow a specific interleaving order, and the pattern repeats every 8 values of $n$.

---

### **Key Observations**

- The pattern of $(x, y)$ coordinates for each $z$ is consistent and cycles every 8 values.
- The sequence of $(x, y)$ for each $z$ is:
  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  (0,0), (1,0), (2,0), \\
  (0,1), (2,1), \\
  (0,2), (1,2), (2,2)
  \end{aligned}
  $$
- This corresponds to the following mapping of $k = n \mod 8$ to $(x, y)$:
  - $k = 0$: $(0, 0)$
  - $k = 1$: $(1, 0)$
  - $k = 2$: $(2, 0)$
  - $k = 3$: $(0, 1)$
  - $k = 4$: $(2, 1)$
  - $k = 5$: $(0, 2)$
  - $k = 6$: $(1, 2)$
  - $k = 7$: $(2, 2)$

---

### **General Formula**

Given an integer $n \geq 0$, the 3D coordinates $(x, y, z)$ are determined as follows:

1. **Compute $z$:**
   $$
   z = \left\lfloor \frac{n}{8} \right\rfloor
   $$

2. **Compute $k$:**
   $$
   k = n \mod 8
   $$

3. **Determine $(x, y)$ based on $k$:**
   - If $k < 3$:
     $$
     y = 0,\quad x = k
     $$
   - If $3 \leq k < 5$:
     $$
     y = 1,\quad x = 2 \cdot (k - 3)
     $$
   - If $k \geq 5$:
     $$
     y = 2,\quad x = k - 5
     $$

---

### **Example**

Let’s compute the coordinates for $n = 11$:

- $z = \left\lfloor \frac{11}{8} \right\rfloor = 1$
- $k = 11 \mod 8 = 3$
- Since $3 \leq k < 5$, then $y = 1$, and $x = 2 \cdot (3 - 3) = 0$

So, $(x, y, z) = (0, 1, 1)$

### **Final Answer**

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&z = \left\lfloor \frac{n}{8} \right\rfloor \\
&k = n \mod 8 \\
&\text{if } k < 3: \quad y = 0,\ x = k \\
&\text{elif } 3 \leq k < 5: \quad y = 1,\ x = 2(k - 3) \\
&\text{else: } y = 2,\ x = k - 5
\end{aligned}
}
