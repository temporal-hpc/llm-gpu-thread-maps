To determine the position of the ant after **1000 moves**, we analyze the movement pattern and derive a general formula for the coordinates after an even number of moves, $ n = 2k $.

---

### **Observation and Pattern Recognition**

From the problem and given examples, the ant moves in a spiral pattern, forming a diamond-like structure. Each side of the square increases in length by 1 for each completed loop. The movement alternates between **right**, **up**, **left**, and **down**.

The key insight is that the position of the ant after an even number of moves, $ n = 2k $, lies on the perimeter of a square with side length $ s $, and we can determine the side and the position on that side based on $ k $.

---

### **Step-by-Step Algorithm**

Let $ k = \frac{n}{2} $, and define the side length of the square as:

$$
s = \left\lfloor \frac{1 + \sqrt{1 + 4k}}{2} \right\rfloor
$$

This gives the largest square with perimeter $ 4s $ that fits within the first $ 2k $ moves.

We then determine the **side** of the square the point lies on:

- **Side 1**: Bottom → x increases from $ s - 1 $ to $ s $
- **Side 2**: Left → y increases from $ s $ to $ s $
- **Side 3**: Top → x decreases from $ s $ to $ s - 1 $
- **Side 4**: Right → y decreases from $ s $ to $ s - 1 $

The exact side is determined by computing the number of steps into the current side.

Let $ r = k - s(s - 1) $, which gives the remaining steps into the current side.

Then:

- If $ r < s $: on **bottom side**, $ (x, y) = (r, 0) $
- If $ r < 2s $: on **left side**, $ (x, y) = (s, r - s) $
- If $ r < 3s $: on **top side**, $ (x, y) = (3s - r, s) $
- If $ r < 4s $: on **right side**, $ (x, y) = (0, 4s - r) $

---

### **Applying the Algorithm for $ n = 1000 $**

1. $ k = \frac{1000}{2} = 500 $
2. $ s = \left\lfloor \frac{1 + \sqrt{1 + 4 \cdot 500}}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{1 + \sqrt{2001}}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{1 + 44.72}{2} \right\rfloor = 22 $
3. $ r = k - s(s - 1) = 500 - 22 \cdot 21 = 500 - 462 = 38 $
4. $ 38 < 2s = 44 $, so the point is on the **left side**.
5. Coordinates: $ (x, y) = (s, r - s) = (22, 38 - 22) = (22, 16) $
