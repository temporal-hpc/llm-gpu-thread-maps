To determine the coordinates (x, y) for a given number \( n \), we can use the following step-by-step approach:

### Step 1: Understand the Pattern
The cumulative sums \( S(y) \) follow a specific pattern based on powers of 2:
- For even \( y = 2k \), \( S(y) = 2^{k+2} - 3 \)
- For odd \( y = 2k + 1 \), \( S(y) = 3 \times 2^{k+1} - 3 \)

### Step 2: Find the Cumulative Sum Range
Given \( n \), determine the smallest \( k \) such that:
\[ 2^{k+2} - 3 \leq n < 2^{(k+1)+2} - 3 \]

This can be found by solving for \( k \) where:
\[ k = \left\lfloor \log_2\left(\frac{n + 3}{4}\right) \right\rfloor \]

### Step 3: Determine the Block
Calculate the starting point of the even block:
\[ \text{start\_even} = 2^{k+2} - 3 \]

If \( n \leq \text{start\_even} + 2^k - 1 \), then it falls in the even block (\( y = 2k \)). Otherwise, it's in the odd block (\( y = 2k + 1 \)).

### Step 4: Calculate x
Once \( y \) is determined:
- If in the even block: \( x = n - \text{start\_even} \)
- If in the odd block: \( x = n - (\text{start\_even} + 2^k) \)

### Example Calculation for \( n = 10 \)
1. Compute \( k = \left\lfloor \log_2(13/4) \right\rfloor = 1 \)
2. Calculate \( \text{start\_even} = 5 \), and the end of even block is \( 5 + 1 = 6 \)
3. Since \( 10 > 6 \), it's in the odd block (\( y = 3 \))
4. Compute \( x = 10 - 6 - 1 = 0 \)

Thus, the coordinates are (0, 4).

### Final Answer
For a given number \( n \), the coordinates are determined by iterating through cumulative sums and calculating \( x \) based on whether \( n \) falls in an even or odd block. 

**Answer:**  
The coordinates for \( n = 10 \) are \(\boxed{(0, 4)}\).
