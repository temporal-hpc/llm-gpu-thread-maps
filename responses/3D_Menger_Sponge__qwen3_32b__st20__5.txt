To compute the 3D coordinates $(x, y, z)$ from a 1D index $n$, we follow a structured approach based on alternating layers of different sizes and internal patterns. Here's a clear and concise breakdown of the method:

---

### **Step 1: Determine the Layer Group**
Each group of layers consists of:
- **Even layers** with 8 elements.
- **Odd layers** with 4 elements.

Each pair (even + odd) contributes a total of 12 elements. We define:
$$
k = \left\lfloor \frac{n}{12} \right\rfloor
$$
This gives the number of full 12-element groups in $n$.

---

### **Step 2: Identify the Sublayer (Even or Odd)**
Within group $k$, the index $n$ can fall in:
- **Even sublayer** (e.g., $z = 2k$) if $n < 12k + 8$
- **Odd sublayer** (e.g., $z = 2k + 1$) if $n \geq 12k + 8$

Compute the **offset within the sublayer**:
- **Even sublayer**:
  $$
  \text{offset\_even} = n - 12k
  $$
- **Odd sublayer**:
  $$
  \text{offset\_odd} = n - 12k - 8
  $$

---

### **Step 3: Compute Coordinates Based on Sublayer**

#### **For Even Sublayers (8 elements)**
Given $\text{offset\_even} \in [0,7]$:
- If $ \text{offset\_even} < 3 $:
  $$
  x = \text{offset\_even}, \quad y = 0
  $$
- Else if $ \text{offset\_even} < 5 $:
  $$
  x = 2 \times (\text{offset\_even} - 3), \quad y = 1
  $$
- Else:
  $$
  x = \text{offset\_even} - 5, \quad y = 2
  $$

#### **For Odd Sublayers (4 elements)**
Given $\text{offset\_odd} \in [0,3]$:
- $$
  y = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{offset\_odd}}{2} \right\rfloor, \quad
  x = 2 \times (\text{offset\_odd} \mod 2)
  $$

---

### **Step 4: Compute $z$**
- For even sublayers:
  $$
  z = 2k
  $$
- For odd sublayers:
  $$
  z = 2k + 1
  $$

---

### **Final Answer Summary**

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&k = \left\lfloor \frac{n}{12} \right\rfloor \\
&\text{if } n < 12k + 8: \\
&\quad \text{offset\_even} = n - 12k \\
&\quad \text{if } \text{offset\_even} < 3: \\
&\quad\quad x = \text{offset\_even},\quad y = 0,\quad z = 2k \\
&\quad \text{elif } \text{offset\_even} < 5: \\
&\quad\quad x = 2 \times (\text{offset\_even} - 3),\quad y = 1,\quad z = 2k \\
&\quad \text{else}: \\
&\quad\quad x = \text{offset\_even} - 5,\quad y = 2,\quad z = 2k \\
&\text{else}: \\
&\quad \text{offset\_odd} = n - 12k - 8 \\
&\quad y = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{offset\_odd}}{2} \right\rfloor,\quad x = 2 \times (\text{offset\_odd} \mod 2),\quad z = 2k + 1
\end{aligned}
}
$$
