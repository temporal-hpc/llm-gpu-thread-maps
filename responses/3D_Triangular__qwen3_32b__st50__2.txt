To determine the coordinates $(x, y)$ of the $n$-th element in a sequence where elements are ordered by the sum $x + y$ (denoted $z$), and for each $z$, elements are ordered by increasing $x$, we follow a structured mathematical approach.

---

### **Step-by-Step Explanation**

1. **Understanding the Sequence Structure**:
   - The sequence is grouped by $z = x + y$, starting from $z = 0$.
   - For each $z$, there are $z + 1$ pairs: $(0, z), (1, z - 1), \dots, (z, 0)$.
   - The number of elements up to and including $z$ is the triangular number:
     $$
     T(z) = \frac{(z + 1)(z + 2)}{2}
     $$

2. **Finding the Correct $z$**:
   - To locate the group where the $n$-th element lies, we solve for the smallest integer $z$ such that:
     $$
     \frac{(z + 1)(z + 2)}{2} \geq n
     $$
   - This is equivalent to finding the smallest integer $m$ such that:
     $$
     \frac{m(m + 1)}{2} \geq n
     $$
     Then, $z = m - 1$.

3. **Finding the Position Within the Group**:
   - Once $z$ is determined, the number of elements before the group $z$ is:
     $$
     T(z - 1) = \frac{z(z + 1)}{2}
     $$
   - The position within the group is:
     $$
     \text{pos} = n - T(z - 1) - 1
     $$

4. **Determining the Coordinates**:
   - The $x$-coordinate is $\text{pos}$.
   - The $y$-coordinate is $z - \text{pos}$.

---

### **Final Formula**

Given a positive integer $n$, the coordinates $(x, y)$ of the $n$-th element are computed as follows:

1. Compute:
   $$
   m = \left\lceil \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rceil
   $$
2. Set:
   $$
   z = m - 1
   $$
3. Compute:
   $$
   T(z - 1) = \frac{z(z + 1)}{2}
   $$
4. Find the position within the group:
   $$
   \text{pos} = n - T(z - 1) - 1
   $$
5. The result is:
   $$
   x = \text{pos}, \quad y = z - \text{pos}
   $$

---

### **Example**

For $n = 7$:

- $m = \left\lceil \frac{\sqrt{57} - 1}{2} \right\rceil = 4$
- $z = 3$
- $T(z - 1) = \frac{3 \cdot 4}{2} = 6$
- $\text{pos} = 7 - 6 - 1 = 0$
- $x = 0$, $y = 3$

✅ The 7th element is $(0, 3)$.

---

### **Final Answer**

$$
\boxed{(x, y) = \left(n - \frac{z(z + 1)}{2} - 1, \, z - \left(n - \frac{z(z + 1)}{2} - 1\right)\right)}
$$
where $z = \left\lceil \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rceil - 1$
