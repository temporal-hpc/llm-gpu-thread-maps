To determine the 3D coordinates $(x, y, z)$ given an integer $n$, we follow a structured approach based on triangular number enumeration in 2D and 3D. Here's the step-by-step explanation:

---

### **Step 1: Determine the $z$-coordinate**
The $z$-coordinate corresponds to the "layer" in a 3D triangular arrangement. For each layer $z$, the number of points in that layer is given by the triangular number:
$$
T_{z+1} = \frac{(z+1)(z+2)}{2}
$$
The total number of points up to layer $z$ is the sum of triangular numbers up to $T_{z+1}$, which simplifies to:
$$
S(z) = \frac{z(z+1)(z+2)}{6}
$$
We need to find the largest $z$ such that $S(z) \leq n$. This can be efficiently done using **binary search**.

---

### **Step 2: Compute the Index Within the Layer ($m$)**
Once $z$ is determined, calculate the offset $m$ within the current layer:
$$
m = n - S(z)
$$
This $m$ represents the position of the point within the 2D triangular grid of the layer.

---

### **Step 3: Determine the 2D Coordinates ($x, y$)**
Within the 2D triangular grid for layer $z$, the points are ordered such that $x + y \leq z$. To find $x$ and $y$ from $m$:

1. **Find the row $s$** such that the sum of points in all rows from $0$ to $s-1$ is less than or equal to $m$. The sum of points in rows $0$ to $s-1$ is:
   $$
   \sum_{i=0}^{s-1} (i+1) = \frac{s(s+1)}{2}
   $$
   Solve for the largest $s$ where $\frac{s(s+1)}{2} \leq m$.

2. **Compute $x$ and $y$**:
   - $x = m - \frac{s(s+1)}{2}$
   - $y = s - x$

---

### **Example: $n = 3$**
1. **Find $z$**:
   - $S(1) = \frac{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}{6} = 1 \leq 3$
   - $S(2) = \frac{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4}{6} = 4 > 3$
   - So, $z = 1$

2. **Compute $m$**:
   - $m = 3 - 1 = 2$

3. **Find $s$**:
   - $\frac{s(s+1)}{2} \leq 2 \Rightarrow s = 1$

4. **Compute $x$ and $y$**:
   - $x = 2 - \frac{1 \cdot 2}{2} = 1$
   - $y = 1 - 1 = 0$

**Result**: $(x, y, z) = (1, 0, 1)$

---

### **Edge Cases**
- **$n = 0$**:
  - $z = 0$, $m = 0$, $s = 0$, $x = 0$, $y = 0$
  - Result: $(0, 0, 0)$

- **$n = 1$**:
  - $z = 1$, $m = 0$, $s = 0$, $x = 0$, $y = 0$
  - Result: $(0, 0, 1)$

---

### **Final Answer**
The coordinates $(x, y, z)$ for a given $n$ are determined by:
1. Finding $z$ such that $\frac{z(z+1)(z+2)}{6} \leq n$.
2. Calculating $m = n - \frac{z(z+1)(z+2)}{6}$.
3. Determining $s$ such that $\frac{s(s+1)}{2} \leq m$.
4. Computing $x = m - \frac{s(s+1)}{2}$ and $y = s - x$.

This method ensures accurate and efficient mapping of $n$ to 3D coordinates in a triangular grid.
