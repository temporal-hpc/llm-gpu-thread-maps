To determine how many times a number can be divided by 2 before becoming odd:

1. **Divide by 2**: Continuously divide the number by 2 until it becomes odd.
2. **Count the divisions**: The count of successful even divisions is your answer.

For example, for the number 4:
- 4 ÷ 2 = 2 (even)
- 2 ÷ 2 = 1 (odd)

**Total divisions: 2 times**

Answer: You can divide by 2 **two times** before it becomes odd.
