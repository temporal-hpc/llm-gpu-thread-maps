To determine the coordinates $(x, y)$ for a given integer $n$ in a specific spiral pattern, we can derive formulas based on the observed behavior of the spiral. Here's a structured approach to compute both $x$ and $y$:

---

### **Step 1: Compute Basic Parameters**
For a given integer $n$:
- **Quotient $q = \left\lfloor \frac{n}{6} \right\rfloor$** (integer division).
- **Remainder $r = n \mod 6$**.
- **Effective quotient modulo 3: $q' = q \mod 3$**.

---

### **Step 2: Compute $y$-Coordinate**
The $y$-coordinate follows a repeating pattern every 6 steps, determined by $q'$ and $r$:

$$
y = 
\begin{cases} 
0 & \text{if } q' = 0 \text{ and } r < 3 \\
\left\lfloor \frac{r - 3}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 & \text{if } q' = 0 \text{ and } r \geq 3 \\
2 & \text{if } q' = 1 \text{ and } r < 3 \\
\left\lfloor \frac{r - 3}{2} \right\rfloor & \text{if } q' = 1 \text{ and } r \geq 3 \\
1 & \text{if } q' = 2 \text{ and } r < 3 \\
2 - \left\lfloor \frac{r - 3}{2} \right\rfloor & \text{if } q' = 2 \text{ and } r \geq 3 
\end{cases}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Compute $x$-Coordinate**
The $x$-coordinate also depends on $q'$ and $r$, but with a different pattern. Observing the spiral's behavior, we can derive:

$$
x = 
\begin{cases} 
\left\lfloor \frac{r}{2} \right\rfloor & \text{if } q' = 0 \\
- \left( \left\lfloor \frac{r}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 \right) & \text{if } q' = 1 \\
\left\lfloor \frac{r}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 & \text{if } q' = 2 
\end{cases}
$$
