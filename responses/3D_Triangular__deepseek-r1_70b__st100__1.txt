To determine the coordinates (x, y) for a given n in the Ulam spiral, follow these steps:

1. **Identify the layer (m):**
   Compute m such that \( T(m) \leq n < T(m+1) \), where \( T(k) = k \times (k + 1)/2 \). This can be found using:
   \[
   m = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
   \]

2. **Calculate t:**
   Determine the position within the layer:
   \[
   t = n - T(m)
   \]

3. **Find l':**
   Compute \( l' \) as the integer part of the solution to the triangular number equation for t:
   \[
   l' = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8t + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
   \]

4. **Determine k:**
   Calculate \( k \) using:
   \[
   k = t - T(l')
   \]
   where \( T(l') = l' \times (l' + 1)/2 \)

5. **Assign coordinates:**
   The coordinates are \( (k, l') \).

**Example for n=4:**

- Compute m:
  \[
  m = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{33} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = 2
  \]

- Calculate t:
  \[
  t = 4 - 3 = 1
  \]

- Find l':
  \[
  l' = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt(9) - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = 1
  \]

- Determine k:
  \[
  k = 1 - 1 = 0
  \]

**Coordinates:** (0, 1)
