To determine the **nth term** of the given sequence, we begin by analyzing the pattern from the provided examples:

---

### **Given Examples:**

| n  | Term |
|----|------|
| 1  | 1    |
| 2  | 1    |
| 3  | 2    |
| 4  | 2    |
| 5  | 3    |
| 6  | 3    |
| 7  | 4    |
| 8  | 4    |

---

### **Observation:**

- The sequence alternates, repeating each integer **twice** before moving to the next.
- The pattern is: **1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, ...**

This suggests that the value of the term increases by 1 **every two positions**. So, the **nth term** corresponds to the **ceiling of n/2**.

---

### **Deriving the Formula:**

We can express the nth term as:

$$
a(n) = \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil
$$

Alternatively, this can also be written using the **floor function**:

$$
a(n) = \left\lfloor \frac{n + 1}{2} \right\rfloor
$$

Both expressions yield the same result for all integer values of $ n $. For example:

- $ a(1) = \left\lceil \frac{1}{2} \right\rceil = 1 $
- $ a(2) = \left\lceil \frac{2}{2} \right\rceil = 1 $
- $ a(3) = \left\lceil \frac{3}{2} \right\rceil = 2 $
- $ a(4) = \left\lceil \frac{4}{2} \right\rceil = 2 $

This matches the pattern perfectly.

---

### **Final Answer:**

$$
\boxed{a(n) = \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil}
$$
