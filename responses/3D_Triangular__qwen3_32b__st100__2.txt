To determine the correct triplet $(x, y, z)$ corresponding to a given index $n$ in a specific ordering of all non-negative integer triplets $(x, y, z)$, we proceed based on the following assumptions and logic:

---

### ✅ Assumptions

1. **Triplet Structure**: Each triplet is of the form $(x, y, z)$, where $x, y, z \geq 0$.
2. **Ordering**: The triplets are ordered first by increasing $z$, then by increasing $y$, and finally by increasing $x$.
3. **Constraint**: For each fixed $z$, we only consider triplets $(x, y, z)$ such that $x + y \leq z$.

---

### 🔢 Key Observations

- For a fixed $z$, the number of valid $(x, y)$ pairs satisfying $x + y \leq z$ is:
  $$
  \sum_{y=0}^{z} (z - y + 1) = \frac{(z+1)(z+2)}{2}
  $$
  This is the $(z+2)$-nd triangular number.

- To find the correct $z$, we accumulate the number of triplets across increasing values of $z$ until the total count exceeds $n$.

- Once $z$ is determined, we find the corresponding $y$ and $x$ by computing how many triplets are present for each $y$ up to $z$, and then computing the position within that $y$-block.

---

### 🧮 Algorithm Steps

Given an index $n$ (1-based), the process is:

1. **Find the value of $z$** such that the sum of the number of triplets for $z = 0$ to $z = k$ is just less than or equal to $n$.
2. **Compute the number of triplets for all $z' < z$**, and subtract that from $n$ to get the position within the $z$-block.
3. **Find the value of $y$** such that the sum of the number of triplets for $y = 0$ to $y = k$ is just less than or equal to the remaining count.
4. **Find $x$** based on the remaining position within the current $y$-block.

---

### 🧠 Example: $n = 10$

1. **Determine $z$**:
   - For $z = 0$: $\frac{(0+1)(0+2)}{2} = 1$
   - For $z = 1$: $\frac{(1+1)(1+2)}{2} = 3$
   - For $z = 2$: $\frac{(2+1)(2+2)}{2} = 6$
   - For $z = 3$: $\frac{(3+1)(3+2)}{2} = 10$

   The cumulative sum up to $z = 2$ is 6, and at $z = 3$, it becomes 10. So, $z = 3$.

2. **Remaining count**: $n - 6 = 10 - 6 = 4$. We now look within $z = 3$.

3. **Determine $y$**:
   - For $y = 0$: $3 - 0 + 1 = 4$
   - The cumulative count up to $y = 0$ is 4, which is equal to the remaining count. So, $y = 0$.

4. **Determine $x$**:
   - For $y = 0$, the valid $x$ values are $0, 1, 2, 3$ (since $x + y \leq 3$).
   - The 4th position corresponds to $x = 3$.

---

### ✅ Final Result

$$
\boxed{(3, 0, 3)}
$$

This is the 10th triplet in the specified ordering.
