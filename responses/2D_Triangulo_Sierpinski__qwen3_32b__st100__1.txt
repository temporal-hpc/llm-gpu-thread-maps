To determine the coordinates of a number $ n $ in the Ulam spiral, follow these steps:

### **Step-by-Step Explanation:**

1. **Find the Layer $ k $:**
   - Identify the integer $ k $ such that $ (2k - 1)^2 < n \leq (2k + 1)^2 $.
   - This $ k $ represents the "layer" of the spiral where $ n $ is located.

2. **Calculate the Side Length $ s $:**
   - $ s = 2k $. This is the side length of the square in the $ k $-th layer.

3. **Compute the Distance $ m $:**
   - $ m = n - (2k - 1)^2 - 1 $. This gives the position of $ n $ within the layer.

4. **Determine the Side and Coordinates:**
   - **Top Side ($ m \leq s $):**  
     $ x = k - m $, $ y = k $.
   - **Right Side ($ s < m \leq 2s $):**  
     $ x = -k $, $ y = k - (m - s) $.
   - **Bottom Side ($ 2s < m \leq 3s $):**  
     $ x = -k + (m - 2s) $, $ y = -k $.
   - **Left Side ($ 3s < m \leq 4s $):**  
     $ x = k $, $ y = -k + (m - 3s) $.

5. **Special Case for Perfect Squares:**
   - If $ n = (2k - 1)^2 $, the coordinates are $ (k - 1, k - 1) $.

---

### **Example:**
**Find the coordinates of $ n = 8 $:**

1. **Find $ k $:**  
   $ (2k - 1)^2 < 8 \leq (2k + 1)^2 $.  
   For $ k = 1 $: $ (2*1 - 1)^2 = 1 < 8 \leq 9 $. Valid.

2. **Calculate $ s $:**  
   $ s = 2*1 = 2 $.

3. **Compute $ m $:**  
   $ m = 8 - 1 - 1 = 6 $.

4. **Determine the Side:**  
   Since $ 3s = 6 $, $ m = 6 $ is on the **left side**.

5. **Calculate Coordinates:**  
   $ x = 1 $, $ y = -1 + (6 - 6) = -1 $.  
   **Coordinates: $ (1, -1) $**.

---

### **Final Answer:**
The coordinates for $ n $ in the Ulam spiral are determined by the layer $ k $, side length $ s $, and position $ m $, as outlined above. For $ n = 8 $, the coordinates are $ \boxed{(1, -1)} $.
