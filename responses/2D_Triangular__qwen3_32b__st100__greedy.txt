To determine the coordinate $(x, y)$ of the $n$-th number in the sequence, we use the following mathematical approach:

---

### **Step-by-Step Explanation**

1. **Understanding the Pattern**:
   - The sequence is arranged in rows where each row $y$ contains $y+1$ elements.
   - For example:
     - Row $y=0$: 1 element → $(0,0)$
     - Row $y=1$: 2 elements → $(0,1), (1,1)$
     - Row $y=2$: 3 elements → $(0,2), (1,2), (2,2)$
     - Row $y=3$: 4 elements → $(0,3), (1,3), (2,3), (3,3)$
     - And so on.

2. **Finding the Row $y$**:
   - The total number of elements up to row $y$ is given by the sum of the first $y+1$ natural numbers:
     $$
     S(y) = \frac{(y+1)(y+2)}{2}
     $$
   - To find the row $y$ that contains the $n$-th number, solve for $y$ in the inequality:
     $$
     \frac{y(y+1)}{2} \leq n < \frac{(y+1)(y+2)}{2}
     $$
   - This simplifies to:
     $$
     y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
     $$

3. **Finding the Column $x$**:
   - Once $y$ is determined, the position within the row is:
     $$
     x = n - \frac{y(y+1)}{2}
     $$

---

### **Final Formula**

Given the index $n$, the coordinate $(x, y)$ is:

$$
y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor
$$
$$
x = n - \frac{y(y+1)}{2}
$$

---

### **Example**

For $n = 6$:

- Compute $y$:
  $$
  y = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8 \cdot 6 + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{49} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{7 - 1}{2} \right\rfloor = 3
  $$
- Compute $x$:
  $$
  x = 6 - \frac{3 \cdot 4}{2} = 6 - 6 = 0
  $$
- Result: $(x, y) = (0, 3)$

---

### **Answer**

The coordinate of the $n$-th number in the sequence is:

$$
\boxed{
\left( n - \frac{y(y+1)}{2}, \left\lfloor \frac{\sqrt{8n + 1} - 1}{2} \right\rfloor \right)
}
